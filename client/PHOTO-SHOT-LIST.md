# TFFC — Photo Shot List
### Everything the website needs, in the order to shoot it.
2498 S Main St, Red Bluff · 530-528-8656

Right now **every interior photograph on the site is a generated stand-in.** They are
honest ones — empty rooms, no invented people, the right kind of building — but they are
not *this* building. Three real photographs of TFFC exist: the exterior, the Wolf Cave
strength floor, and the Kids Fit room. This list replaces the rest.

## 0. Before anybody picks up a camera

| | |
|---|---|
| **Releases** | Adults: a one-line model release for every recognisable face. **Children: written parental consent, no exceptions** — including the existing Kids Fit photo, which we are holding until it has one. |
| **Format** | Landscape 3:2 for rooms. **Vertical 4:5 for portraits** (the team pages are built to that crop). Shoot RAW if you can, biggest JPEG if you can't. |
| **Lens** | Wide for rooms — 16–24mm equivalent. A phone's 0.5× ultrawide is genuinely fine. Portraits at 50–85mm equivalent, not the ultrawide. |
| **Height** | Rooms at chest height, camera level. Tipping up or down makes the walls lean and small rooms look smaller. |
| **Lights** | Every light on, in every room, including the ones nobody turns on. Blinds open. |
| **Tidy first** | Weights racked, cables straight, mats squared, bins emptied, cords tucked, nothing personal on the floor. Ten minutes of tidying beats an hour of retouching. |
| **Don't fix** | Scuffs, wear, an older machine next to a new one. That is what makes it look like a real gym instead of a render. |

**Nobody's phone number, nobody's face, no member's belongings in frame without asking.**

## 2. Staff portraits — 17 people, and the team pages are waiting on them

There is now a page for every person: `/team/` plus `/team/<name>/`. Each already carries
that person's real sessions off the live calendar. **The only thing missing is the face.**
Until then everyone shows a navy monogram — deliberately, because a stock face or a
generated one on a named person in a town of fourteen thousand is not survivable.

**Setup — shoot all 17 the same way or they will not read as a set:**
- **Vertical 4:5.** Head and shoulders to mid-chest. Eyes about a third down the frame.
- **One background for everybody.** The plain drywall by the studio, or the corridor wall. Not the equipment.
- **Stand them 4–5 feet off the wall** so the background falls soft and they don't cast a hard shadow on it.
- **Face the window light.** No flash, no overhead-only — the ceiling troffers alone make raccoon eyes.
- **Two frames each: one looking at camera, one mid-laugh.** The second one is almost always the keeper.
- Gym clothes. What they actually teach in.

## 7. Do not shoot

- Any child without written parental consent. Any adult who has not signed.
- Anyone's phone screen, locker contents, or bag.
- Brand names filling the frame — a machine badge in passing is fine, a logo as the subject is not.
- Empty rooms *lit only* by the overhead troffers at night. They go green and grim.
- The pool. **There is no pool at 2498 S Main** — the "3 Lane Indoor Pool and Spa" on your
  current website is the PT clinic's, next door at 2490. That listing needs correcting.
