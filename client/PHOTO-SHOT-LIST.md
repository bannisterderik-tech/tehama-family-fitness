# TFFC — Photo Shot List
### What we need for the website.
2498 S Main St, Red Bluff · 530-528-8656

Landscape, lights on, rooms tidy. Twenty shots.

---

## 1. The list

- **The building from the parking lot** — wide, late afternoon or golden hour
- **The building at dusk**, windows lit from inside
- **The front entry** — gable, round window, glass doors, straight on
- **The front desk**, from inside the door, the way a member sees it
- **The lobby** — seating, noticeboard, window light
- **The Fuel Bar counter** — blender, coffee, fridge, menu board
- **The strength floor** — down the line of new machines, wall lettering in frame
- **The Olympic platform**, bar loaded, low angle
- **The free weight room**, wide, corner to corner
- **The women's weight room**, from the door, so it reads as its own room
- **The cardio floor**, wide, down the row of machines
- **The cardio theater**, screens lit, room lights down
- **The circuit training room**, the machines in their ring
- **The basketball court**, wide from a corner to the far hoop
- **The racquetball court**
- **The pickleball courts**, nets up, lines visible
- **The main studio** — mirrored wall and barre, from the back corner
- **The spin room**, down the rows of bikes
- **The kids' room**, wide
- **The locker room and sauna door**

---

## 2. Also worth getting

- **A portrait of everyone who works there.** Same wall, same light, one at a time.
  Vertical, head and shoulders, gym clothes. Two frames each — one looking at the camera,
  one mid-laugh.
- **One class actually running**, shot from the back of the room.
- **The early crowd**, first thing in the morning.

---

## 3. Two rules

- **No child in a photo without a parent's written OK.** Adults sign a one-liner too.
- **Send the original files.** No filters, no crops, no edits.
