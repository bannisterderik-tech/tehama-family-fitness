Tai chi at 7:15. Five mornings a week. Every week.

Standing the whole time, slow weight-shifting forms, easy on the joints and genuinely hard on your balance — which is the point. It is the most consistently scheduled thing in this building.

The pool

care about the weather

Three indoor lanes and a spa. For anyone working around a knee, a hip or a back that will not tolerate a treadmill, this is the most forgiving room in the county — and it is open in August and in January alike.

Getting started

It takes one visit

  1. 01 Bring your card to the front deskWhoever is on will take it from there. If you are not sure your plan includes SilverSneakers, call first and ask — the desk handles this every week.
  2. 02 Come a few minutes early to your first classTell the instructor it is your first one. Tai chi at 7:15 is the easiest place to start.
  3. 03 Use the rest of the buildingThe pool, the spa, the sauna, the cardio deck and the weight floor are all part of it.
